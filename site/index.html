Does your wallet hold a share?

Every eligible wallet had the same chance. If your address was selected, claim your coins — the contract checks your share itself, from the sealed seed and your address. Nothing was sent to a wallet that did not ask, and unclaimed coins are burned when the window closes.

Connect your wallet to check.

Fair start

The redistribution goes only to wallets holding between 0.1 and 40 ETH (about one bitcoin's worth) — those who missed Bitcoin's early years, not the already-wealthy. Above the floor, wallet size buys no better odds.

Self-verifying

Each claim is recomputed on-chain from the sealed seed and your address. There is no posted recipients list to trust and no operator after genesis.

Sealed once

A single random value, fixed at genesis after the eligible list is committed, decides every share. Nobody — the author included — can choose who receives.

Honest about the rest

The eligible list is built by a public, reproducible rule; anyone can re-derive and compare it. Unclaimed coins are burned. The coin may be worth nothing.
